Privacy Policy for Baldic Download Interceptor

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Baldic Download Interceptor ("the Extension") is a companion extension designed to work with the Baldic Download Manager desktop application.

Data Collection and Use

  • We do not collect, store, or share any personal user data.
  • The Extension only scans active web pages locally in your browser to detect downloadable media streams and links when explicitly requested by you.
  • Detected links are sent exclusively to your local desktop application running on your machine (localhost:8080) to initiate downloads. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Permissions

  • activeTab & scripting: Used locally to inspect page elements and retrieve media URLs.
  • downloads: Used to assist in handling resource links.
  • webRequest & Host Permissions: Used to detect and list downloadable streams on the active pages you visit.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository.
